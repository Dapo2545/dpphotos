Questions

Frequently asked

How far in advance should we book?

Weddings are usually booked 6–12 months ahead; portraits and events 2–4 weeks ahead. Reach out anyway — last-minute dates do open up.

Do you travel for shoots?

Yes. I'm based in Lagos and regularly photograph across Nigeria and internationally. Travel costs are quoted transparently up front.

When will we receive our photos?

You'll get a sneak peek within 48 hours. Full portrait galleries arrive within 2 weeks, and wedding galleries within 4–6 weeks.

How do we secure our date?

A signed agreement and a 50% retainer reserve your date. The balance is due one week before the shoot.
