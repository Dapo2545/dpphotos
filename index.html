Questions

Frequently asked

How far in advance should we book?

Portrait and studio shoots are usually booked 1–2 weeks ahead; events and graduations 2–4 weeks ahead. Reach out anyway — last-minute dates do open up.

Do you travel for shoots?

Yes. I'm based in Abuja and regularly photograph across Nigeria and beyond. Travel costs are quoted transparently up front.

When will we receive our photos?

You'll get a sneak peek within 48 hours. Your full set of edited photos arrives within 1–2 weeks.

How do we secure our date?

A 50% deposit reserves your date. The balance is paid on the day of the shoot.
